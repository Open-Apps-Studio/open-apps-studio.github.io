Mental Math Support

(Open Source) Mental Math is a free mental arithmetic trainer with timed tests, custom drills, 48 tricks, Magic Square puzzles and progress tracking.

Common Fixes

  • Update to the latest App Store version.
  • Restart the app if a drill appears stuck.
  • Check Settings inside the app for theme and haptic preferences.
