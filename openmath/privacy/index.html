Mental Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Mental Math is a mental arithmetic practice app from Open Apps Studio. It is designed to work without accounts, ads, analytics, or subscriptions.

Data Collection

Mental Math does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information. Practice settings, favorites, and progress are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and scores.

Third Parties

Mental Math does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or analytics services.

Children

Mental Math is a general learning tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Open Apps Studio through the Mental Math support page.
